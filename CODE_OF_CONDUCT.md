# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome different experience levels. Critique changes rather than contributors. Do not harass others, disclose personal information or post discriminatory or threatening material.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact method they publish. The owner should publish that method before inviting community participation.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces.
